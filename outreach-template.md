# Cold Outreach Email Templates

## Local Service Businesses (Plumbers, Roofers, HVAC, Electricians, Attorneys)

**Subject line options:**
- How many leads did you lose this month?
- Your next job is out there
- Quick question about [city] [service]

---

## Key principles:

1. **Subject lines** that create curiosity or name a problem — not "AI tools" or "automation"
2. **Keep it short** — 4-5 sentences max
3. **No jargon** — speak like a person, not a software salesperson
4. **Low commitment ask** — "15-minute call" not "sign up for a demo"
5. **Local specificity** — city, service type, real company name shows you did the work

## Firehose targeting:

Target businesses with:
- No intake form on their website (form submissions come to their email)
- No clear "book now" or "get a quote" path
- Service-area businesses that rely on phone/Google leads
- Businesses with Google Business Profile (they're already getting leads, just losing them)

Search queries for Firehose:
- `site:[city].google.com "plumber" "open today"` (find active local plumbers)
- `site:[city].google.com "HVAC" "heating repair"` (find active HVAC companies)
- Industry directories with no online booking

## Next step after outreach:

- Add them to a simple follow-up sequence
- Schedule a 15-min call to close
